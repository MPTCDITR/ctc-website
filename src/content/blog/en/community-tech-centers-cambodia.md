Community Tech Centers (CTCs) are creating new opportunities for students, youth, and local residents across Cambodia by providing access to computers, high-speed internet, and digital learning resources.

By bringing technology closer to communities, CTCs help ensure that everyone regardless of location, has the opportunity to participate in Cambodia’s growing digital economy. The 21 CTCs can contribute as below:

- Improve digital literacy;
- Expand access to technology;
- Support lifelong learning;
- Enable digital entrepreneurship; and
- Reduce the urban-rural digital divide.

Empowering communities with digital knowledge is one of the most effective ways to create inclusive economic growth and build a resilient digital society. When communities are connected, they become stronger, smarter, and more resilient.

Join in building a stronger community and learn more on the official CTC website at www.ctc.edu.kh to follow the latest updates.

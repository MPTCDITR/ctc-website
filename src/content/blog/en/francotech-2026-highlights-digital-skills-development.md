FrancoTech 2026: Showcasing Digital Skill Development Opportunities Through Community Tech Centers

During the three-day FrancoTech 2026 event, officials from the Ministry of Post and Telecommunications (MPTC) participated to introduce and showcase activities, training programs, and digital skill development opportunities to participants and visitors regarding the Community Tech Center (CTC) project.

Through this exhibition, participants learned more about the role of CTCs in providing digital skills learning and training opportunities to strengthen capacity and prepare for the demands of the digital economy.

This activity also served as a valuable opportunity to promote the importance of digital skills and raise awareness of learning opportunities and digital human resource development in Cambodia.

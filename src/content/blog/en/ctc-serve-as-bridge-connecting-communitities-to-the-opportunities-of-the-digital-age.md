Community Tech Centers (CTC) serve as a bridge connecting communities to the opportunities of the digital age, bringing technology, knowledge, and digital learning resources closer to people across Cambodia.

Through its network of 21 centers, students, youth, and local residents can access opportunities to learn, strengthen their digital skills, explore new knowledge, and develop innovative ideas for the future. More than just technology access points, CTCs serve as a foundation for:

- Strengthening digital literacy and capabilities;
- Expanding access to technology and information;
- Promoting lifelong learning;
- Encouraging digital entrepreneurship and innovation; and
- Bridging the digital divide between urban and rural communities.

We believe that building a strong digital society begins with empowering people and ensuring equal access to opportunities. When communities are equipped with knowledge, skills, and technology, they become better positioned to participate in and contribute to Cambodia’s digital transformation.

Learn more: www.ctc.edu.kh

A Great Opportunity for 2026 High School (Bac II) Graduates Receiving Overall Grades A, B, and C

Have you passed the 2026 Upper Secondary School Examination (Bac II) with overall grades A, B, or C and wish to pursue a bachelor’s degree in digital skills? Have you inquired about the "Digital Skills Development Program (DSDP)"—a interest-free student loan program available across 7 partner universities?

Please note that the deadline for application submission is October 31, 2026. You can visit the information desks at any of the 7 partner universities, which include:

Cambodia Academy of Digital Technology (CADT)

Institute of Technology of Cambodia (ITC)

Royal University of Phnom Penh (RUPP)

National University of Management (NUM)

American University of Phnom Penh (AUPP)

Paragon International University

University of Cambodia (UC)

Successful candidates of the DSDP will receive the following benefits:

Full tuition coverage until the completion of a bachelor’s degree with zero interest (Note: This is a loan, not a scholarship)

Funds to purchase a laptop (if needed)

Funds for housing/rental expenses (if needed)

Academic, housing, and career development guidance

Excellent job opportunities and competitive salaries in both the public and private sectors

Additional benefits provided by each respective higher education institution

Applicants must fill out the online application via the link: https://go.gov.kh/mptc/dsdp-registration.

For further information, please contact via phone number 123.
